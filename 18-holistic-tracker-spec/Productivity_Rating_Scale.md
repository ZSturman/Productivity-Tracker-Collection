# Productivity Rating Scale

## Extremely Productive

- Activities that directly contribute to major goals and involve high mental or physical engagement.
- **Rating**: 8-10

## Very Productive

- Activities that contribute significantly to goals but may not involve as much engagement.
- **Rating**: 6-7

## Moderately Productive

- Activities that contribute to goals but at a lower intensity or frequency.
- **Rating**: 4-5

## Slightly Productive

- Activities that contribute indirectly to goals or to a small extent.
- **Rating**: 2-3

## Neutral

- Activities that neither contribute to nor detract from goals.
- **Rating**: 0

## Unproductive Levels

- **Slightly Unproductive**
  - Activities that slightly detract from goals.
  - **Rating**: -2 to -3
- **Moderately Unproductive**
  - Activities that detract from goals and consume moderate time.
  - **Rating**: -4 to -5
- **Very Unproductive**
  - Activities that significantly detract from goals.
  - **Rating**: -6 to -7
- **Extremely Unproductive**
  - Activities that greatly detract from goals and consume excessive time.
  - **Rating**: -8 to -10

*For psychological aspects, refer to [Motivation Theory](Motivation_Theory.md).*
