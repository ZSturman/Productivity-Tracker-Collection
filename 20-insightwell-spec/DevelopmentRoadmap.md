# Development Roadmap

## Phase 1: Core Development (Months 1-3)

- **Build Essential Features**
  - Implement mood tracking, health metrics, and basic data logging.
- **Develop Basic Visualizations**
  - Create simple charts and graphs for immediate user feedback.
- **Establish Database Infrastructure**
  - Set up secure data storage and management systems.

## Phase 2: Advanced Analytics (Months 4-6)

- **Implement Advanced Visualizations**
  - Introduce interactive and multi-dimensional data representations.
- **Integrate Statistical Analysis Tools**
  - Enable users to perform deeper analysis of their data.
- **Enhance Data Import/Export**
  - Allow synchronization with external devices and platforms.

## Phase 3: Social Integration (Months 7-8)

- **Community Features**
  - Launch social sharing options and community advice forums.
- **Challenges and Competitions**
  - Introduce collaborative activities and friendly competitions.

## Phase 4: Premium Features (Months 9-10)

- **Launch Pro Version**
  - Offer advanced analytics and personalized coaching.
- **Merchandise Integration**
  - Provide physical products like wellness journals and pens.

## Timelines and Milestones

- **Month 1:** Project initiation and team assembly.
- **Month 2:** Completion of initial design prototypes.
- **Month 3:** Release of Minimum Viable Product (MVP) for beta testing.
- **Month 6:** Full deployment of core features.
- **Month 8:** Integration of social features.
- **Month 10:** Launch of premium features and pro version.
