# Core Features and Functionality

## Data Collection Modules

- **Mood Tracking**
  - Daily mood logs with customizable scales.
- **Physical Health Metrics**
  - Exercise routines, sleep quality, nutrition intake.
- **Mental Well-being Indicators**
  - Stress levels, mindfulness practices, meditation frequency.
- **Cognitive Performance Tests**
  - Assessments for focus, memory, and cognitive agility.
- **Financial Health Tracking**
  - Monitor income, expenses, savings, and debt levels.
- **Relationship Quality Assessments**
  - Evaluate time spent and quality of interactions with others.
- **Environmental Data Capture**
  - Record weather conditions, noise levels, and air quality.
- **Digital Habits Monitoring**
  - Track screen time and app usage statistics.

## User Interaction Features

- **Daily Tasks and Challenges**
  - Personalized activities to promote healthy habits.
- **Social Sharing and Community Advice**
  - Share progress and receive feedback from a supportive community.
- **Goal Setting**
  - Set, track, and achieve daily and long-term goals.
- **Skill Learning Modules**
  - Access resources for personal and professional development.
