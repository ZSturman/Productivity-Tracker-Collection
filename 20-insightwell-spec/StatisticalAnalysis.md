# Statistical Analysis and Metrics

## Descriptive Statistics

- **Mean, Median, Mode**
  - Measure central tendencies within datasets.
- **Range and Variance**
  - Understand the spread and dispersion of data.

## Distribution Analysis

- **Skewness and Kurtosis**
  - Analyze the symmetry and peakedness of data distributions.
- **Standard Deviation**
  - Quantify the amount of variation in a set of data values.

## Correlation and Regression

- **Covariance and Correlation Coefficients**
  - Assess the strength and direction of relationships between variables.
- **Regression Models**
  - Predict values of a dependent variable based on independent variables.

## Time Series Analysis

- **Moving Averages**
  - Smooth out short-term fluctuations to highlight longer-term trends.
- **Trend Analysis**
  - Identify consistent upward or downward patterns over time.
- **Seasonality Decomposition**
  - Separate data into trend, seasonal, and residual components to understand periodic fluctuations.

## Advanced Metrics

- **Quantile Rankings**
  - Determine where a particular value falls within its data distribution.
- **Growth Rates**
  - Measure the rate at which a variable increases or decreases over time.
- **Outlier Detection**
  - Identify data points that significantly deviate from other observations.
