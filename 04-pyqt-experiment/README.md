## The Story

Three days after the most ambitious branch, this one goes in the other direction. It's more experimental and less complete. That's useful to know, because it says something important about how this project actually moved: not in a straight line.

A newer date didn't always mean a more finished result. I was still testing directions, exploring what felt right at this scale, and not every experiment was meant to replace what came before. This version exists because sometimes you have to build a thing to confirm it's not the thing you want to build.

Progress across this archive is real, but it's not linear. This branch is one of the earliest reminders of that.

## What It Does

A lighter desktop application experiment exploring an alternative approach to the productivity suite concept. Less structured than the May 28 build, with fewer views and a simpler data model.

## What I Learned

That not every iteration needs to be the most complete version. Sometimes you build something to test an idea quickly, and the test itself — even if the result is "this isn't it" — is the value. This branch confirmed that the May 28 direction was stronger, which is its own kind of progress.
