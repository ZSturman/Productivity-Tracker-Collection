## Brain Control Dynamics

### Cortex

- **Control**: Believes it's in control, creates scenarios to reinforce this belief.
- **Shift in Focus**: Noticeable shift when engaging with preferred stimuli (e.g., songs, TV shows).

### Limbic System

- **Actual Control**: More in control than the cortex.

### Lizard Brain

- **Response**: Instinctual responses like fear and flight.

---

## Brain Fatigue

### How to Overcome It

- **Strategy**: Engage the limbic system, which is less energy-intensive than the cortex.

### Cortex Tasks

- Thinking about future goals
- Starting tasks like coding

### Limbic Tasks

- Worrying about finances and living situations
- Feeling rewarded for productivity and learning

### Lizard Brain Responses

- Instinctual fear and basic survival responses

---

## Focus and Retention Factors

### Cortex

- **Influences**:
    - Interest
    - Length of engagement
    - Medium of information
    - Teacher's effectiveness
    - Prior knowledge
    - Distractions
    - Future plans with the knowledge
    - Priority relative to other tasks

### Limbic System

- **Influences**:
    - Confidence
    - Current mood
    - Stress levels
    - Location

### Lizard Brain

- **Influences**:
    - Health
    - Perception of danger
    - Shelter
    - Diet
    - Sleep
    - Breathing
    - Chemical influences
